# 💫 About Me:
Hi, I’m Rama Dhani – a passionate visual storyteller based in Indonesia.<br><br>I specialize in photography, videography, and the artistic exploration of light as a medium. My work blends creativity and technical precision to capture powerful emotions, candid moments, and compelling narratives — from portraits and events to commercial shoots.<br><br>Driven by curiosity and inspired by everyday beauty, I believe every frame has a story to tell. Whether it’s through a single photo or a cinematic sequence, I aim to connect people with visuals that resonate, inspire, and endure.<br><br>When I’m not behind the lens, I’m constantly learning — exploring new techniques, experimenting with light, and evolving my creative voice.<br><br>Let’s collaborate and tell something unforgettable.
